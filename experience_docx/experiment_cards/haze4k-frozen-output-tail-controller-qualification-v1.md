## Scientific rationale

The archived 3x3 observable representation-by-learner factorial decisively failed under one shared corrected tilewise controller, but that veto makes the zero-gain cell outcomes non-diagnostic about raw low-risk-tail quality. Independent evidence shows apparently useful global risk ranking alongside approximately 25 percent harm in the selected low-risk tail, so average AUC and Brier score are not adequate safety evidence. This orthogonal development route reuses the receipt-bound 9-cell, 5-fold, 3-seed outputs without retraining ConvIR or the heads. It verifies all 10,285 upstream files and their 5.23 GB SHA inventory, reconstructs seed and ensemble predictions from the frozen checkpoints and scene caches, and confines probability fitting and controller selection to the fixed 150-scene calibration role. Weighted q, exact p=q/(2-q) for applicable L0/L1 heads, scene-balanced Platt and isotonic probability maps, and the historical one-sided correction are compared with raw and corrected utility. A tilewise controller is the control; the substantive mechanism is a scene-level setwise selector whose selected prefix must satisfy a predicted 0.10 area-weighted risk budget. One candidate is hash-frozen before formal OOF arrays are parsed. All nine cells then receive complete 600-scene risk-coverage, harm, gain, fold, and seed evaluation; the selected candidate receives exact image replay, uniform, shuffle, joint permutation, mixed-GT, GT/GT, and 100-scene nested test-development stress controls. Confirmation, canary, locked-test, NH-Haze, ITS, and OTS remain unread.
