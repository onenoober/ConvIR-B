## Scientific rationale

The archived observable-tail factorial preserved substantial privileged headroom but every one of its nine representation-by-learner cells selected zero action and zero gain. This orthogonal development route removes the calibrated utility/risk veto and learns one keep/weaken/strengthen policy directly from counterfactual action replay. The joint arm trains a route-specific observable encoder and soft decision objective that combines material utility and tail harm; a parameter-matched separate utility/risk arm tests whether joint decision learning is the active mechanism, and a full-data base-feature arm tests whether the learned encoder is necessary. Within each of five original-clear-scene outer folds, nested 120/240/360/480-scene subsets and three paired seeds produce a frozen learning curve. A disjoint 150-scene role supplies only the pre-training target-feasibility certificate, and 100 historical development scenes supply only four-variant target-stability diagnostics. The 600-scene OOF candidate keeps the existing gain, activation, selected-area harm, and precision thresholds. The paired 75-to-100 percent slope diagnoses data limitation but cannot relax or override the primary gate. Candidate-confirmation, canary, locked-test, NH-Haze, ITS, and OTS remain unread.
