## Scientific rationale

The completed signed measurement established materially prevalent overshoot in high-demand regions, but low-demand overshoot was descriptively at least as large. This direct within-source comparison freezes the existing region and overshoot definitions, averages hazy variants within each clear-source group, and estimates the paired high-minus-low difference with a fixed group bootstrap. It performs no training, threshold selection or protected-data access.
