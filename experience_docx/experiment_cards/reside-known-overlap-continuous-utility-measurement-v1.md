## Scientific rationale

The Haze4K continuous-utility precision pilot validly blocked its 100-scene population because the conservative planning SD 0.444219 dB implied about 1,213 independent scenes for the unchanged 0.025 dB precision distance. It did not test the mean or reject the continuous target. Independently archived quarantine routes now authorize design on 8,813 ITS and 8,006 OTS clear scenes after removal of verified Haze4K exposure. This orthogonal target-domain route preserves the original two-action continuous PSNR utility contrasts, taper, 0.05 dB gate, 0.025 dB precision distance, official checkpoint, no-training rule and original-clear-scene unit. It reports ITS and OTS separately and uses their maximum mean as the primary estimand; no sample-size pooling treats indoor and outdoor scenes as exchangeable. To cover both simultaneous stratum claims at familywise 95 percent, a Bonferroni critical value 2.241403 with the archived planning SD requires 1,587 independent scenes per stratum. Four haze variants per scene are deterministic nested repeats and never increase n. The failed ITS q-region construct is excluded rather than repaired or reinterpreted.
