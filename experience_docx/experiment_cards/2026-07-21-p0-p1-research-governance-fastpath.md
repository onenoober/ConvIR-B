# P0/P1 Research Governance Fastpath

Date: 2026-07-21

Status: adoption authorized; effective when the accepted implementation and
closeout reach GitHub `main`.

## Purpose

Reduce repeated documentation, validation, workspace and engineering-
qualification work without weakening scientific contracts or making a closed
route family a dead end for the wider research problem. This is generic
infrastructure and is not tied to one restoration model or dataset.

## Accepted Scope

- program-defined adjacent budgets with orthogonal and evidence-backed reopen
  paths;
- one experiment spec compiled deterministically into schema-6 route assets;
- bounded/redacted failure diagnostics and tightly receipt-bound engineering
  discard;
- isolated clean route workspaces;
- a deterministic policy read index that never replaces full authority files;
- exact-identity reuse of engineering capability only.

Historical manifest schemas 4 and 5 remain supported. The MCP keeps protocol
schema 4 and exactly six tools. Scientific permission, protected-data access,
promotion and deployment never transfer through capability reuse.
