## Post-Hoc Findings

- The primary selected policy contains `730/768` no-op operator-image rows.
- Of `609` repairable operator-image rows, `577` abstain; `32` act, and `12` of
  those active rows select a non-oracle action.
- All `181` negative-oracle operator-image rows receive zero negative actions.
- Fold 0 has zero coverage; fold 1 has `9.8958%` coverage. Both operators agree
  on the qualitative failure, and their mean gains differ by only about
  `0.000047 dB`.
- The `38` active rows have mean `+0.004357 dB`, but median `+0.001411 dB`,
  p10 `-0.011817 dB`, CVaR5 `-0.041943 dB`, and `47.37%` negative outcomes.
  Removing the top 10% active gains makes the active-row mean negative.
- Under descriptive forced coverage, severe groups rise from `0/39` at 10% to
  `4/77` at 20%, `12/231` at 60%, and `41/384` at 100%; hard groups first
  appear at 60% and reach `9/384` at 100%.
- The negative-full candidate is substantially riskier than positive-full in
  this evaluated subset, but the primary still contains meaningful
  candidate-conditioned risk information beyond action-only and state-only
  controls. This narrows, rather than proves, the generic-risk hypothesis.

## Evidence Gaps

The formal run did not persist per-seed predictions, per-action mean/q05
utility score vectors, composite risk-coverage confidence, region rows or
semantic/fog subgroup labels. Therefore:

- per-seed stability is unavailable;
- the formal risk-coverage ordering cannot be independently replayed;
- E1 component substitution cannot be executed as originally drafted;
- sky, highlight, texture, distance, edge, color and fog-severity claims cannot
  be made;
- missing values must not be regenerated by retraining or inference.
